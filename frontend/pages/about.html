Our Heritage & Story

A Pillar of Cycling Culture in Bulacan Since 1989

Taurus Bike Shop is an independent bicycle and components retailer situated along Sandico Street, Abangan Sur, Marilao, Bulacan. Established in 1989, our enterprise has continuously served the local cycling community for approximately 37 years.

Officially registered under the leadership of Rowell Lu Caisido, with Russel Lu Caisido serving as co-owner, Taurus Bike Shop has grown from a humble local workshop into a comprehensive destination for cycling gear, pre-built bikes, and top-tier bicycle mechanics.

Our customer base primarily consists of students, daily commuters, workforce employees, and seasoned cycling enthusiasts. We understand that whether a bike is used for a daily commute to school or work, a high-octane trail adventure, or a long-distance weekend ride, riders depend on their bicycles for safety, fitness, and reliable mobility.

Our Mission

To deliver dependable, authentic bicycles and mechanical components that empower riders of all backgrounds—students, daily commuters, and sports cyclists alike. We strive to provide transparent diagnostics, high-standard maintenance, and long-term customer relationships built on integrity and mechanical precision.

Our Vision

To stand as Bulacan's most trusted independent cycling institution, seamlessly blending hands-on workshop heritage with modern digital ordering solutions (BICOBS / TaurOS) to keep communities moving smoothly, sustainably, and safely.

Our Core Pillars

Built on Trust, Precision, and Community

100% Authenticity

Every component, frame, brake set, and drivetrain in our catalog is sourced from verified suppliers to ensure total durability and safety on the road.

Mechanical Expertise

Our skilled mechanics carry out hands-on inspections, wheel truing, tune-ups, and assemblies with rigorous attention to detail and 30-minute to 4-hour turnarounds.
